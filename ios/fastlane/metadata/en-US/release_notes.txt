New
- Hear how an answer is pronounced right inside the quiz, so you no longer need to switch to a dictionary app.
- Listen to just the paragraph around the blank you are solving. You hear only the part you need instead of the whole proposal, which makes long English text less tiring to follow. Blanks you have not answered yet are never read aloud, so the answer is not given away.
- Speech no longer stops your music or podcasts. The volume dips while it speaks and returns when it finishes.
