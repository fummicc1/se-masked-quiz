New
- Filter the proposal list by status, so you can focus on just Implemented, Active Review, or any other status.

Improvements & Fixes
- Answer masked quizzes in two steps: pick a choice, then tap Next. The text scrolls to the next blank for you, so there is no need to hunt for it.
- Moved the choices into a sheet at the bottom of the screen. You can read the proposal while answering, and buttons no longer shift the moment you make a selection.
- Added a solved/total counter to the quiz screen so you can see how many questions are left.
- Made it easier to spot which choice was correct after answering.
- "Prerequisite proposals" can now be collapsed, giving the proposal text more room.
- Fixed masked quizzes being unusable with VoiceOver. Blanks are now announced as buttons, and results are spoken aloud.
